Year-by-Year Roadmap

A structured timeline for students aiming for GATE 2027.

Year 1 (Foundation Phase)

Focus completely on Engineering Mathematics and General Aptitude. Start with 1 core subject of your branch.

Year 2 (Core Building)

Complete 60-70% of the core subjects. Solve previous year questions (PYQs) simultaneously. Make detailed notes.

Year 3 (Intense Practice)

Finish remaining syllabus by August. Start subject-wise test series. Revise everything using short notes.

Final 3 Months (Mock Phase)

Full-length mock tests every 3 days. Focus entirely on revision, mistake analysis, and time management.
